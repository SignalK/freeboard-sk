![Fig 1. A plugin's search panel open alongside its toolbar button](extension-panels-1.jpg)

Panel extensions let a Signal K server plugin add a completely new feature to
Freeboard, without waiting on a Freeboard code change to get it. You can pick
and choose which of these tools you want installed on your boat. Each one
adds its own new button to Freeboard's toolbar — for example, a proximity
search for nearby points of interest, or an automatic route planner that
routes around land.

![Fig 2. An auto-routing panel, another example of a panel extension](extension-panels-2.jpg)

**To use one**, tap the grid icon in the top-right corner of the chart to
reveal the toolbar, then tap the plugin's button. Its panel slides in from
the right-hand side of the screen. Tap the button again, or use the panel's
own close button, to put it away. Some plugins show their panel in a floating
window instead — see below.

A panel can also narrow down what's shown on the chart — for example,
filtering the waypoints list to just the ones matching your search. When a
panel does this, a small chip appears near the top of the screen describing
the filter; tap its **×** to clear it and show everything again.

## Floating windows

![Fig 3. A plugin's window floating over the chart](extension-panels-3.jpg)

A plugin can also show its panel in a window that floats over the chart,
instead of in the side panel. That lets you keep something like a depth
sounder or a camera feed on screen while you navigate, and several windows can
be open at once. A window may open from the plugin's toolbar button, or the
plugin may open it by itself.

- **Move** a window by dragging its title bar. Dropped close to an edge of the
  chart, it snaps flush to that edge.
- **Resize** it by dragging any edge or corner.
- **Collapse** it to just its title bar by double-clicking the title, or with
  the **^** button. Do the same again to expand it.
- **See through it**: tap **⋮** and choose an opacity of 100%, 80%, 60% or 40%.
- **Close** it with **✕**. Some plugins show **−** instead. That hides the
  window but leaves the plugin running, so a sounder, for example, keeps
  recording its history. How you bring it back is up to the plugin.

Some windows hide their title bar when it is idle, to give the panel more
room. Point at, or tap, the top-center edge of the window to bring the bar
back.

A **modal** window dims the chart behind it until you dismiss it, with its
**✕** or the Escape key.

Windows never cover the toolbars, the Alarms button or alarm messages. On a
narrow screen such as a phone, a window opens as a sheet along the bottom of
the screen instead, and a modal window fills the screen.

Freeboard does not reopen windows after a reload; the plugin reopens them if
it wants to. Some plugins remember where you left a window and how big it was,
and reopen it the same way on that device.

If you haven't installed any plugins that add a panel, the extension toolbar
button doesn't appear — Freeboard looks and works exactly as it always has.
