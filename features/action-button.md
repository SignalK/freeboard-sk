The Action Button is the large round button in the bottom-right corner of the
chart. It is the one control that is always in the same place and always in
reach, so it holds whichever single action you want fastest on your boat.
Choose it in **Settings → Display → Action Button**:

- **Waypoint at vessel** — marks your boat's current position. It opens the
  new-waypoint dialog with the position filled in, ready to name and save. It
  is greyed out until Freeboard knows where the vessel is.
- **Person Overboard** — a red button that raises a Person Overboard alarm on
  the Signal K server, so every device connected to it sounds the alarm.
- **Autopilot Console** — opens and closes the autopilot console. It lights
  up while the autopilot is engaged, and is greyed out unless the server
  reports an autopilot.
- **Radar Overlay** — shows the radar on the chart and opens its control
  panel. It lights up while the radar is showing, and is greyed out unless a
  radar has been found.
- **A plugin's button** — any button a plotter extension plugin adds, listed
  by the plugin's name and the button's title. It works and looks exactly as
  it does on the toolbar, including any count, highlight or warning color
  the plugin gives it.

Autopilot, Radar and plugin buttons normally sit on the right-hand toolbar.
Whichever one you choose moves to the corner instead, so it never appears
twice. On a phone, where a long toolbar pushes buttons into the **…** menu,
this is how to keep the one you use most in sight.

## Things to know

- If you choose a plugin's button and the plugin is later disabled or
  uninstalled — or the plugin hides that button for a while — the corner is
  left empty rather than switching to something else. Your choice is kept,
  and the button comes back by itself when the plugin does. Meanwhile
  Settings lists it as **Not available**.
- The Action Button moves up out of the way when screen widgets occupy the
  bottom-right corner.
- It is hidden while you draw or measure on the chart, and in kiosk mode.
