title: Following a Route
category: Navigation

![Fig 1. Tap a route and choose Start to start following it](route-following-1.jpg)

Start following a route and Freeboard steers you along it, point by point —
and when plans change underway, you can rejoin it further on or skip a point
without starting over.

**Following a route.** Tap a route on the chart and choose **Start**, or press
**Start** beside it in the Routes list or in its info panel, and Freeboard
starts following it from its first point. To start partway along, press
**Start here** beside a point in the info panel's point list, or open the
route's **Points** and choose **Start at nearest point** to pick up the route
at the point closest to your boat.

**Following a route you haven't saved.** For a quick "take me there" route —
around a shoal, or into an anchorage — you don't have to name and save it
first. Tap **Start** on a freshly drawn route, or **Start here** on one of its
points, and Freeboard starts following it at once. It stores the route on your
server as **Temporary route**, and its info panel labels it **(temporary)**.
It stays on the chart after you stop following it, so if the course gets
cleared — a detour to a waypoint, or the autopilot or another display clearing
it — just press **Start** on it again. Only one temporary route is kept: it's
replaced the next time you start following a drawn route, and removed a day
after you stop following it. To keep it for good, press **Save** (*Keep this
route*) in its info panel and give it a name; it becomes an ordinary route.

![Fig 2. The course controls while following a route — previous and next point, reverse, restart XTE and Course Settings](route-following-2.jpg)

**Moving along the route.** While a route is active, the course controls
offer **Previous point** (⏮) and **Next point** (⏭). Both steer straight from
where your boat is to the new point, then follow the route on from there.
Alongside them, **Reverse route direction** follows the route back the other
way, **Restart XTE** measures cross-track error from your current position,
and **Course Settings** opens the course options.

**Moving the course panel.** The panel holding these controls, with the
course data beside them (VMG, bearing, cross-track error, distance and time
to go, ETA), opens in the lower left corner of the chart. If it hides
something you need, drag it somewhere else: press anywhere on it that isn't
a button — a reading, or the "2 of 9" point count — and move it. On a
touchscreen, hold your finger on it until it lifts (it gains a shadow, and
buzzes on devices that can), then drag; a quick swipe still scrolls the
panel sideways when its readings don't fit. It stays near whichever corner
you leave it by when the screen resizes or turns, and it reopens there the
next time a course is active. The position is remembered on each device
separately, so a tablet and a laptop can each keep it where it suits them.

![Fig 3. Clicking a point on the route you're following — Rejoin here, or Skip to the point after it](route-following-3.jpg)

**Rejoining or skipping a point.** Click a point on the route you're following
— the point itself, not a leg — and a popover names it ("3 of 4") with its
position. **Rejoin here** steers straight to that point and then follows the
route on from it: handy after a detour, or to cut a corner. On the point
you're heading for, **Skip** steers straight to the point after it. On a
day-sail loop, the marina is the first point on the way out and the last on
the way home. The route's info panel offers the same actions: **Rejoin here**
beside each point, and **Skip this point** beside the one you're heading for.
Both are unavailable while the route has unsaved edits.

**Arriving at a point.** When you reach a point, an alert offers **Next
point**. To move on without touching anything, turn on **Auto-advance to next
point on arrival** in Settings → Course, then choose which notification
triggers it (passing the perpendicular, or entering the arrival circle) and
how long to wait: **Immediately**, or 1 to 30 seconds. While it waits, the
alert counts down ("Next point in 5 secs", or "End Route in 5 secs" at the
last point). Press the countdown to cancel, and the alert offers **Next
point** (or **End Route**) for when you're ready. With **Immediately** there
is no countdown: the next point is selected, or the route ended, as soon as
the alert arrives. Advancing on arrival, by hand or automatically, follows the
planned leg rather than steering straight from the boat. The **Arrival
Circle** size is set in the same place.

**Stopping.** **Stop Navigating** ends navigation. It's beside the active
route in the Routes list and its info panel, in the route's popover, in the
More Actions menu, and in the chart's right-click menu.
