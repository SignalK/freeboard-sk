![Fig 1. A phone held sideways: the left toolbar's hidden buttons at the top of More actions](toolbar-overflow-1.jpg)

Freeboard's left and right-hand toolbars always fit on the screen. When there's room for
every button, you see them all. When there isn't — a phone turned sideways, a small
Raspberry Pi screen, or a right-hand toolbar filled up by extension plugins — each toolbar
shows as many buttons as fit and puts the rest in a menu. No button is ever pushed off
the edge of the screen.

## Where the hidden buttons go

![Fig 2. The right-hand toolbar's … menu](toolbar-overflow-2.jpg)

- **Right-hand toolbar:** a **…** button appears in the last space. Tap it to see the
  buttons that didn't fit.
- **Left toolbar:** the **More actions** button moves to the last space, and the buttons
  that didn't fit are listed at the top of its menu, above its usual items.

Each menu entry shows the button's icon and name, and choosing it does exactly what the
button does. Layers and Draw open their own menus alongside. Tap anywhere else, or tap
**…** again, to close the menu.

The buttons keep their usual order, and the ones at the bottom of a toolbar are the first
to move into the menu. So Alarms, at the top of the left toolbar, is the last button to
go, and on the right-hand toolbar the buttons added by extension plugins move before
Freeboard's own.

## Nothing switched on is out of sight

When a hidden button is switched on or carries a count — radar on, Heading Up, Follow
Vessel, alarms — the **…** or **More actions** button shows a small dot. In the menu, a
switched-on button's icon is highlighted, and a count follows its name, for example
"Alarms (3)".

## Things to know

- The toolbars rearrange as soon as the window changes size, such as when you rotate a
  tablet.
- The right-hand toolbar stops above the Action Button in the bottom-right corner,
  including when screen widgets push that button up.
- While you're drawing or measuring, **More actions** still opens if it holds a button you
  can use, but its usual items wait until you finish.
